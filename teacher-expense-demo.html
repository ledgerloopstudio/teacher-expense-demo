Help

Is this tax advice?
No. The Tax Helper is an organizational summary of your own records. Tax rules vary — always confirm deductions with a qualified tax professional.
Where is my data stored?
Only in this browser on this device (localStorage). No account, no cloud, no tracking. Use Backup (JSON) before switching devices or clearing browser data.
Can I attach receipt photos?
The app is fully offline, so it stores a receipt note (e.g. "Envelope #3" or "photo IMG_2041") rather than image files. Keep the physical or phone photo alongside the note.
How do reimbursements work?
Tick "Reimbursable" when logging an expense, then bundle those expenses into a claim on the Reimbursements tab. Advance the claim Submitted → Approved → Paid as the office processes it.
